# Introduction

The Introduction series sets the tone for your journey into Competitive Programming. It covers what CP is, why it matters, how to think like a problem solver, and how to approach contests and practice effectively. You'll get familiar with the tools, platforms (like Codeforces and AtCoder), and the overall roadmap. This part also introduces mindset topics like contest stress, building a cheatsheet, and managing your learning resources.
