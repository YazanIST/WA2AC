# Level 1 – Core Algorithms

Level 1 introduces core algorithmic techniques that every problem solver must know. It includes prefix sums, binary search, two pointers, recursion, and backtracking. You'll also explore the Sieve of Eratosthenes, basic graph traversal (DFS/BFS), bit manipulation, and complete search. The problems at this level move beyond implementation and begin to test your logic and efficiency.
